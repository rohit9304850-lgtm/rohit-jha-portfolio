HOW TO RUN
1. Keep all files in the same folder.
2. Open index.html in a browser.
3. To enable the Resume button, place your own resume PDF in this folder and name it resume.pdf.

BEFORE PUBLISHING
- Check your email, phone, location and social profile links in index.html.
- Check that the education dates and all listed skills accurately reflect your current details.
- The Projects section has been left unchanged.
